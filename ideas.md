The problem
Joining a bank is not like joining most companies.
Vetting and screening mean weeks, sometimes months, between an unconditional offer and the first day, and the first 30 days decide whether someone settles in or drifts. Workday records the joining process; it doesn't guide anyone through it. New starters struggle to know what comes next, find what's relevant to their role and location, and meet their manager and buddy before they arrive. And it all stops at day one.
