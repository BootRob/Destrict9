# Our purpose

> Synthetic document for the LaunchPad prototype. Not real policy. The purpose below is made up for this prototype.

Reading this before you start helps you understand why the work you'll do matters. It takes about 5 minutes.

## Why the Bank exists
Our purpose is **to help people and communities build a secure future**. Everything we do should make it easier for customers to manage money day to day, plan for big moments like buying a home, and get through hard times.

## What that means in practice
- **For customers:** clear products, fair prices, and help that's easy to reach when something goes wrong.
- **For communities:** supporting small businesses, first-time buyers and local projects in the places where we work, including Leeds and Cardiff.
- **For colleagues:** a place where you can learn, be yourself and build a career.

## How your role connects to it
Whatever your job, you'll have a part in this:
- Engineers build the apps and systems customers rely on every day.
- Contact centre colleagues are often the voice of the Bank when a customer needs help most.
- Analysts help us understand what customers need and where we can do better.

Your manager will talk to you in your first week about how your team's work supports our purpose.

## Our long-term commitments
- Make banking simpler and more inclusive, so nobody is left behind as services move online.
- Help customers build financial resilience, for example by encouraging saving and spotting signs of money worries early.
- Reach net zero in our own operations, and help customers make greener choices.
