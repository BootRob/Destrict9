# Our values

> Synthetic document for the LaunchPad prototype. Not real policy. The values below are made up for this prototype.

Our four values describe how we work with customers and with each other. They're part of how everyone's performance is discussed, alongside what they deliver. Reading this takes about 5 minutes.

## 1. Put people first
Think about the customer or colleague on the other side of every decision. Listen before you act, and treat people as you'd want your own family to be treated.
*In practice:* taking the extra minute to check a vulnerable customer understands what happens next.

## 2. Do the right thing
Be honest, fair and open, even when it's harder. If something doesn't look right, speak up (see *Code of conduct essentials*).
*In practice:* flagging a mistake you made as soon as you notice it, rather than hoping nobody sees.

## 3. Keep it simple
Make things easy to understand and easy to use, for customers and colleagues. Cut steps that don't add value.
*In practice:* writing a customer letter in plain English, or removing a form field nobody needs.

## 4. Grow together
Share what you know, ask for help, and support each other to learn. Celebrate other people's wins.
*In practice:* pairing with a new colleague, or asking a question in a team channel so others can learn from the answer.

## How values show up in your first 30 days
- Your induction includes a short session on the values.
- At your 30-day check-in, your manager will ask how you've seen the values in action, and where they could be lived better.
