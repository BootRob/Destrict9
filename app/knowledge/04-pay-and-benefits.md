# Pay and benefits

> Synthetic document for the LaunchPad prototype. Not real policy.

## Pay
- You're paid **monthly, on the 20th**. If the 20th falls on a weekend or bank holiday, you're paid on the working day before.
- Your first payslip covers the days you've worked since your start date. If you start after the 10th of the month, your first pay is in the following month's payroll, together with that month's pay.
- Payslips are in the HR portal from your first day. Your bank details must be entered in the onboarding portal **at least 10 working days before your first pay date**.
- The salary in your offer letter is your gross annual salary before tax, National Insurance and pension.

## Holiday
- **25 days' holiday** a year plus bank holidays, pro rata for part-time colleagues and for your first holiday year.
- The holiday year runs January to December. You can carry over up to 5 unused days.
- You can buy up to 5 extra days through flexible benefits.
- Holiday you booked before your offer will be honoured. Tell your recruiter or manager the dates as early as possible.

## Pension
- You're automatically enrolled in the Bank's pension scheme.
- The Bank contributes **up to 12%** of your salary when you contribute 6%. The default is 6% from you and 12% from the Bank.
- You can change your contribution, or opt out, in the HR portal at any time.

## Flexible benefits
Your benefits window opens when your checks clear and stays open until 30 days after your start date. Options include:
- Buying or selling holiday.
- Private medical insurance (core cover is free; you can add family members).
- Cycle to work scheme.
- Dental insurance.
- Discounted gym membership.
If you don't make choices, you get the core benefits automatically. You can change them again in the annual window each December.

## Other things worth knowing
- **Bonus:** most roles are eligible for a discretionary annual bonus, based on the Bank's and your own performance. Your first year's bonus is pro rata.
- **Share scheme:** you can join the share save scheme in the next annual window after you start.
- **Expenses** are claimed in the HR portal and paid with your next salary. See *Hybrid working, locations and travel*.
