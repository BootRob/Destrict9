# Your journey: from offer to Day 30

> Synthetic document for the LaunchPad prototype. Not real policy.

Joining the Bank happens in six stages. You can be in more than one at once, and the app shows where you are.

## 1. Offer
Getting to an unconditional offer takes three steps:
1. **Conditional offer.** Your recruiter offers you the job, subject to pre-employment checks. You accept or decline it in the app.
2. **Screening.** You enter your details on the secure website of ClearPath Screening, the external company that runs your checks, then confirm in the app that you've done it. The app never asks for your personal details. This usually takes 2 to 4 weeks (see *Pre-employment checks*). The app shows that your checks are in progress, even when there's no news.
3. **Unconditional offer.** When your checks clear, your recruiter records the result and sends you an unconditional offer to accept in the app.

Your recruiter is your main contact until you've accepted your unconditional offer. If you answer the screening questions honestly, there's very rarely a reason for checks to stop an offer.

## 2. Unlock and choose
Once you've accepted your conditional offer, the onboarding portal unlocks. In this stage you:
- Upload your right-to-work and identity documents (see *Pre-employment checks*).
- Enter your bank details for pay and your emergency contact.
- Choose your benefits. The benefits window opens when your checks clear and stays open until 30 days after your start date.
- Tell us about any workplace adjustments you need (see *Wellbeing, adjustments and support*).
- Do the pre-start onboarding training, about 40 minutes. Finish it before Day 1 and your first lunch with the team is on us (see *Learning and mandatory training*).

## 3. Meet your people
You'll be introduced to two people:
- **Your recruiter** looks after your offer and your checks. You can message them from the start.
- **Your hiring manager** looks after your role, team, start date and first weeks. Your chat with them opens when you accept your unconditional offer, and they'll message you to agree your start date.

## 4. Manager ready
About a week before you start, your manager confirms your first-week plan, orders your equipment and books your induction. Your recruiter hands you over to your manager once you've accepted your unconditional offer.

## 5. Day 1 to Week 1
Your first day is always a Monday (or Tuesday after a bank holiday). Week 1 is mostly setup, induction and meeting your team. See *Your first day and first week*.

## 6. Day 30
By Day 30 you should have finished your mandatory training, had a 30-day check-in with your manager and agreed your first objectives. See *Learning and mandatory training*.
