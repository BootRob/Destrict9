# Your journey: from offer to Day 30

> Synthetic document for the LaunchPad prototype. Not real policy.

Joining the Bank happens in six stages. You can be in more than one at once, and the app shows where you are.

## 1. Offer
You've accepted an unconditional offer. Your recruiter is your main contact until your pre-employment checks clear. Expect a welcome email within 2 working days with links to the onboarding portal.

## 2. Unlock and choose
Once your checks start, the onboarding portal unlocks. In this stage you:
- Upload your right-to-work and identity documents (see *Pre-employment checks*).
- Enter your bank details for pay and your emergency contact.
- Choose your benefits. The benefits window opens when your checks clear and stays open until 30 days after your start date.
- Tell us about any workplace adjustments you need (see *Wellbeing, adjustments and support*).

## 3. Meet your people
You'll be introduced to three people:
- **Your recruiter** looks after your offer, checks and start date.
- **Your hiring manager** looks after your role, team and first weeks.
- **Your buddy** is a colleague in a similar role who helps with the everyday things. Buddies are usually assigned 2 to 3 weeks before your start date.

## 4. Manager ready
About a week before you start, your manager confirms your first-week plan, orders your equipment and books your induction. Your recruiter hands you over to your manager once your checks clear.

## 5. Day 1 to Week 1
Your first day is always a Monday (or Tuesday after a bank holiday). Week 1 is mostly setup, induction and meeting your team. See *Your first day and first week*.

## 6. Day 30
By Day 30 you should have finished your mandatory training, had a 30-day check-in with your manager and agreed your first objectives. See *Learning and mandatory training*.
