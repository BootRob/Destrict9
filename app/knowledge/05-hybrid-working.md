# Hybrid working, locations and travel

> Synthetic document for the LaunchPad prototype. Not real policy.

## How hybrid working works
- Most office-based roles spend **at least 2 days a week** in the office, averaged over a month. Your team agrees which days, so you're in at the same time as your colleagues.
- Contact centre and branch roles are based in a location, with set shifts. Your manager confirms your rota before you start.
- In your **first 4 weeks**, expect to be in the office more often (usually 3 to 4 days a week) so you can meet people and learn quickly.

## Booking a desk
- Offices use hot desking. Book a desk, or a car park space in Leeds, in the **desk booking app** on your laptop or phone. You can book up to 2 weeks ahead.
- On your first day, a desk near your team is booked for you.
- Teams have "neighbourhoods": areas of desks set aside for them on their office days. Your manager will show you yours.
- If you've agreed a workplace adjustment, such as a specific chair or screen, you'll get a fixed desk instead.

## Working from home
- You'll need a safe, private space where customer and colleague information can't be seen or overheard.
- The Bank provides a laptop, headset and, if you ask, a monitor, keyboard and mouse. Order these through your manager.
- You can claim a one-off **£150 home working allowance** in your first 3 months for a chair or desk. Keep the receipt.

## Travel and expenses
- Travel between home and your usual office isn't paid. Travel to a different office or to meet customers is.
- Book business travel through the travel portal, and choose standard-class rail.
- Claim expenses within 60 days, with receipts, in the HR portal.

## Flexible working
You can ask for flexible working (for example compressed hours or part-time) from your first day. Talk to your manager first, then apply in the HR portal. You'll get a decision within 2 months.
