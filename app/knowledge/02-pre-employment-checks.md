# Pre-employment checks

> Synthetic document for the LaunchPad prototype. Not real policy.

Every new colleague goes through pre-employment screening before they start. It's a regulatory requirement for banks, not a judgement about you.

## Who runs the checks
Checks are carried out by an external screening company, **ClearPath Screening** (fictional). They'll email you a link to their secure portal. Emails from them come from `@clearpath-screening.example`. If you're unsure whether an email is genuine, ask your recruiter before clicking anything.

## What's checked
- **Identity and right to work in the UK.** Passport, or another accepted document from the list in the portal.
- **Employment history** for the last 3 years, including any gaps longer than 3 months. A short explanation of a gap (travel, study, caring) is fine.
- **Basic criminal record check** (DBS in England and Wales).
- **Credit and financial check.** This looks for county court judgments and bankruptcy, not your credit score. It does not affect your credit rating.
- **Regulatory references** for roles covered by the financial services regulators. Your recruiter will tell you if this applies to your role.

## How long it takes
Usually **2 to 4 weeks**. The most common delays are a referee who hasn't replied, or a document that's unclear. You can see progress in the ClearPath portal, and your recruiter gets the same updates.

## If something comes up
Answer every question honestly. Having something on your record doesn't automatically stop you joining. If there's something you'd like to explain, tell your recruiter early. The screening team looks at the circumstances, and you'll always get the chance to discuss it before any decision.

## When checks clear
Your recruiter confirms your start date in writing, usually within 1 working day of your checks clearing. You can't start until all checks are complete. If they're still open less than 5 working days before your start date, your start date may move (see *When plans change*).
