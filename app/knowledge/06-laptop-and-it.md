# Your laptop, accounts and IT

> Synthetic document for the LaunchPad prototype. Not real policy.

## Before you start
- Your manager orders your laptop and phone (if your role needs one) about a week before you start.
- Everyone starts in the office: your laptop is set up with you on Day 1, so it isn't posted to you.
- You won't get access to Bank systems before Day 1. **Nobody from the Bank will ever ask for your password**, before you start or after.

## Day 1 setup
- Collect your laptop from the IT Welcome Desk in your office. Bring your building pass.
- You'll set up your account password and multi-factor authentication (MFA) using the authenticator app on your phone. If you'd rather not use your personal phone, ask for a hardware security key.
- Setup takes about an hour. The IT Welcome Desk stays with you until you can sign in to email, Teams and the HR portal.

## Getting help
- **IT Service Desk:** available 24/7 through the "IT Help" button on your laptop, or by phone (the number is on the back of your building pass).
- Lost or stolen laptop or phone: report it to the IT Service Desk **straight away**, at any time of day.

## Using your own devices
You can use the Teams and Outlook apps on your personal phone once you've set up MFA. Don't forward work email to personal accounts, and don't store work files on personal devices.
