# Getting to the office: travel and parking

> Synthetic document for the LaunchPad prototype. Not real policy. Office details are made up for this prototype.

Your welcome email gives your office's exact address and who will meet you. Plan to arrive by **9:30am** on your first day.

## Leeds office
- **By train:** about a 10-minute walk from Leeds railway station. Follow signs for the city centre business district.
- **By bus:** several city centre routes stop within 5 minutes' walk.
- **Parking:** the office has a small car park with **40 spaces**, booked through the desk booking app the day before. Spaces are limited, so most colleagues use public transport. Blue Badge spaces are kept free and don't need booking.
- **Electric vehicles:** 6 charging points on level 1, bookable like normal spaces.
- **Cycling:** secure bike storage, lockers and showers on the ground floor.

## Cardiff office
- **By train:** about a 5-minute walk from Cardiff Central station.
- **By bus:** the main bus interchange is next to the station.
- **Parking:** there's **no colleague parking** at the Cardiff office. Several public car parks are nearby; the Bank doesn't cover their cost for your normal commute.
- **Cycling:** secure bike storage and showers in the basement.
- **Shift workers:** if your contact centre shift starts before 7am or ends after 9pm, ask your manager about the late-travel taxi scheme.

## Your first day
- You won't have a building pass yet, so go to reception with your photo ID (see *Your first day and first week*).
- If you're driving to the Leeds office on your first day, ask your manager to book a car park space for you, because you won't have the booking app until your laptop is set up.
- If you've told us you need adjustments (for example a Blue Badge space or step-free access), your manager will confirm the arrangements before you start.

## Travel costs
Your normal commute isn't paid for. Travel to a different office or to meet customers can be claimed (see *Hybrid working, locations and travel*). You can join the season ticket loan scheme from your first day: the Bank lends you the cost of an annual rail or bus ticket, and you repay it from your salary over 12 months.
