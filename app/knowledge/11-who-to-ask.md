# Who to ask

> Synthetic document for the LaunchPad prototype. Not real policy.

| Question about | Ask |
|---|---|
| Your offer, salary in the offer, contract | Your **recruiter** |
| Your conditional or unconditional offer, pre-employment checks, documents | Your **recruiter** |
| Agreeing your start date | Your **hiring manager**, once you've accepted your unconditional offer |
| Notice period, changing your start date | Your **recruiter** |
| Your role, team, objectives, first-week plan | Your **hiring manager** |
| Equipment, hybrid working pattern, rota | Your **hiring manager** |
| Workplace adjustments | Your **recruiter** before you start, your **hiring manager** after |
| Day-to-day things: where things are, how things really work, who's who | Your **hiring manager**, who can introduce you to the team |
| Payslips, pension, benefits, HR portal | **HR Service** (through the HR portal) |
| Laptop, accounts, passwords, MFA | **IT Service Desk** |

## After your unconditional offer
Your recruiter hands you over to your hiring manager, and your chat with them opens. You can still message your recruiter about anything to do with your contract or start date.

## Response times
Your recruiter and manager aim to reply within **1 working day**. For anything urgent on your first day, call the number in your welcome email.
