# Code of conduct essentials

> Synthetic document for the LaunchPad prototype. Not real policy.

This is a short summary. You'll do the full Code of conduct module in your first 30 days.

## Conflicts of interest
Tell your manager about anything that could conflict with your work at the Bank, for example a second job, a directorship, or a close relative who works for a supplier. Most second jobs are fine once they're declared and approved.

## Personal investments and trading
Some roles have rules about buying and selling shares. You'll be told in Week 1 if your role is covered. Until then, don't trade in the Bank's own shares.

## Gifts and hospitality
Don't accept gifts or hospitality worth more than **£50**, and record anything you're offered in the gifts register, even if you decline it.

## Social media
You can say you work at the Bank. Don't share customer information, confidential information, or photos taken inside offices, and make it clear that your views are your own.

## Customer and colleague data
Only look at customer or colleague data you need for your job. Never send it to personal email or devices.

## Speaking up
If you see something that doesn't feel right, raise it with your manager or use the confidential Speak Up line. You're protected from any detriment for raising a genuine concern.
