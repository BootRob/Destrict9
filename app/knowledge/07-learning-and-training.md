# Learning and mandatory training

> Synthetic document for the LaunchPad prototype. Not real policy.

## Mandatory training
Everyone completes these modules in the learning portal **within 30 days of starting**. Together they take about 6 hours, and you do them in work time.
- Code of conduct (45 minutes)
- Data protection and information security (1 hour)
- Financial crime: anti-money laundering, sanctions and fraud (1.5 hours)
- Treating customers fairly and Consumer Duty (1 hour)
- Health, safety and wellbeing (30 minutes)
- Inclusion and respect at work (45 minutes)
- Speaking up (whistleblowing) (30 minutes)

Your manager can see your progress. If you can't finish in 30 days (for example because of leave), talk to them.

## Role training
Your manager will agree role-specific training with you in Week 1. Customer-facing roles have a structured training programme for the first 4 to 6 weeks before taking customer calls or appointments.

## Your 30-day check-in
Around Day 30 you'll have a check-in with your manager to:
- Talk about how your first month has gone.
- Confirm your mandatory training is complete.
- Agree your first objectives for the year.
Your probation period is **6 months**, with check-ins at 1, 3 and 6 months.

## Before you start: pre-start onboarding training
The **pre-start onboarding training** is a short course in the onboarding portal, about 40 minutes in total. It covers:
- Our purpose and our values (see *Our purpose* and *Our values*).
- How your first day works, and getting to the office (see *Your first day and first week* and *Getting to the office*).
- A short quiz at the end.

It's optional, and it isn't part of the mandatory training above, but it's worth doing: **complete it before your first day and your Day 1 lunch with your team is on us.** Your manager can see whether you've finished it. Your manager may also suggest some reading for your role.
