# Wellbeing, adjustments and support

> Synthetic document for the LaunchPad prototype. Not real policy.

## Workplace adjustments
If you have a disability, long-term condition or neurodivergence, you can ask for adjustments, for example assistive software, a specific desk set-up, adjusted hours or a quiet space.
- Tell us in the onboarding portal, or tell your recruiter or manager. You don't have to share a diagnosis.
- Ask as early as possible so things are ready for Day 1. Most adjustments are in place within 2 weeks.
- The information you share is kept confidential and only used to arrange your adjustments.

## Employee Assistance Programme
Free, confidential support 24/7 for you and your household: counselling, money and legal advice, and help with caring responsibilities. You can use it from your first day. Details are in the HR portal.

## Feeling unwell
If you're unwell and can't work, tell your manager before your usual start time, by phone or message. Record the absence in the HR portal when you're back. You'll need a fit note from a doctor after 7 calendar days.

## Colleague networks
Networks for gender, ethnicity, LGBTQ+, disability, carers, faith, armed forces and early careers are open to everyone. You can join from your first day through the intranet.

## Family and caring leave
Enhanced parental leave and paid carer's leave are available from your first day. See the HR portal for details, or ask your manager.
