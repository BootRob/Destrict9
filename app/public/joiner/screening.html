Nothing to worry about. Everyone who joins a bank is screened. It's run by an independent company, and the most important thing is to answer every question honestly.

We don't collect your personal details in this app. You give them straight to ClearPath, our screening partner.

How it works

  1. Go to ClearPath. Their secure site opens in a new tab.
  2. Complete your details there. It takes about 15 minutes.
  3. Come back and tap "I've added my details". We'll show you how your checks are going.

Have these ready

  • Your passport or photo driving licence
  • Your addresses for the last 5 years
  • Your employers (or studies) for the last 3 years, with dates
  • Your National Insurance number
