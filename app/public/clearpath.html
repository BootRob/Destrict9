CPClearPath Screening

Pre-employment screening

Simulated third-party site. In the real journey, this is ClearPath's own secure website, where the candidate enters their details. Lloyds Banking Group's app never sees them. Nothing is collected here.

On the real site you would provide:

  • Identity documents (passport or driving licence)
  • Address history for the last 5 years
  • Employment or study history for the last 3 years
  • National Insurance number and consent to the checks

If this tab doesn't close, switch back to the app and tap I've added my details.
